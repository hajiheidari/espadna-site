How to play

  • Place exactly one crown in every row.
  • Place exactly one crown in every column.
  • Place exactly one crown in every color region.
  • Crowns never touch, not even diagonally.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The web version is completely free and ad-free. The mobile app is free with ads, and you never see an ad in the middle of a puzzle.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up; your progress stays on your own device.

What if I get stuck?

Tiaro’s hints first show you where to look, then explain why, and can make the move for you.

Which languages?

English, Arabic and Persian. Switch in the game’s Settings.
