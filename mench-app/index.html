How to play with a friend

  1. Tap Play with friends and then Create room.
  2. Tap Share invitation and send the link on WhatsApp, Telegram or any messenger.
  3. Your friend opens the link and taps Join room; no app or account needed.
  4. When everyone is in, the host taps Start game. Lost the connection? Open the game again and you are back in your seat.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, with no purchases or sign-up. The only ads are optional ones in the app’s shop, watched for free coins.

Are the dice fair?

In online games the server commits to each roll before you roll and your device adds its own random number, so every result can be checked under “Dice history”. The bot never changes the dice.

Can I install it?

On Android open the Chrome menu ⋮ and choose “Install app”; on iPhone tap ⬆️ in Safari and choose “Add to Home Screen”.
