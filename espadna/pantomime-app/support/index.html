Quick answers

Heads Up does not react when I tilt the phone

Hold the phone on your forehead in landscape and tilt clearly down (correct) or up (pass). If your phone has no motion sensor, use "Start with Buttons".

The camera or video does not work

Recording is optional. Turn it on in Settings ⚙️ and allow camera and microphone access. If you denied it before, allow it in your phone's Settings app → Dorehami.

Where are my photos and videos?

They stay on your phone. Tap Save to keep them in your gallery or Share to send them.

How do I change the language?

Settings ⚙️ → Language: English, Persian, Arabic, Russian or Turkish.
