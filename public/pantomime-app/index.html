How to play

  1. Make teams and pick how long each turn lasts.
  2. One player sees the word and acts it out without speaking.
  3. Teammates guess: correct ✅ or pass ⏭️.
  4. In forehead mode, hold the phone on your forehead: tilt down for correct, up to pass.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. Ads never interrupt a game. If you like, watch one short ad (or share the game with friends) to unlock the bonus word categories for 24 hours.

Do I need the internet?

No. The app works fully offline. New words arrive by themselves whenever you are online.

Which game modes are there?

Pantomime: the classic game, one word per turn, 30 s to 2 min, the other team can pick the word. Speed Pantomime: act out as many words as you can before time runs out. Heads Up: the phone on your forehead while your friends act it out.

How many players can play?

2 to 4 teams, with as many players as you like in each team.

Which languages does it support?

English, Persian (فارسی), Arabic (العربية), Russian and Turkish, each with its own word packs. Change the language any time in Settings.

Can we take photos and videos?

Yes. Before the game, take a group photo in one of 5 fun frames with the date and your own caption, story-sized. You can also film any pantomime turn or record Heads Up rounds. Photos and videos stay on your phone.

Can I play without installing?

Yes, the browser version works on any phone (button above), but the app is smoother and has all the features.
