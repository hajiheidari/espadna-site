How to play

  1. Make teams and pick how long each turn lasts.
  2. One player sees the word and acts it out without speaking.
  3. Teammates guess: correct ✅ or pass ⏭️.
  4. In forehead mode, hold the phone on your forehead: tilt down for correct, up to pass.

Add to your home screen

iPhone (Safari)

Tap the share button ⬆️ and choose “Add to Home Screen”. For forehead mode, allow “Motion & Orientation” the first time.

Android (Chrome)

Open the ⋮ menu and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Open the game once; after that it runs without internet. The word list updates by itself whenever you are online.
