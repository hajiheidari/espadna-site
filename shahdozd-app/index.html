Shah, Thief & Minister

The classic party game of Iran and India, for one phone.

Four modes

Persian Classic4 players

King, Minister, Thief and Executioner. The Minister has one guess.

Indian Classic4 players

Raja, Mantri, Chor and Sipahi, with both local rules for who finds the Chor.

Joker Edition5 players

The Joker pretends to be the thief and wins big if the Minister falls for it.

Two Thieves6 players

Two thieves are hiding. The Minister picks two players: one thief is enough to win.

Made for the table

One phone, secret roles

Each player presses and holds the card to see their role. Let go and it hides.

Rules always on screen

A short rule for the current mode stays at the top while you play.

Scores and tournaments

Traditional 1000/800/500 points or simple party scoring, with round history.

No internet, no forced ads

Names and scores stay on your device. No account. Ads only if you choose to watch one for coins.

How a round goes

  1. The phone goes around; everyone secretly sees their role.
  2. The King and the Minister reveal themselves.
  3. The Minister points at the thief.
  4. Cards flip, points are added — next round in seconds.
