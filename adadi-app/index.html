How to play

  • Every row and every column is an equation.
  • Take a number from the tiles and put it in an empty box.
  • The equations cross: each number must work both ways.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution. No guessing, just math.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The web version is completely free and ad-free. The mobile app is free with ads between levels, never in the middle of a puzzle, and one purchase removes them.

Who is it for?

Ages 13 and up, with three age groups and four difficulty levels in each.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up; your progress stays on your own device.

Which languages?

English, Arabic, Persian and Urdu. Switch language and digit style in the game’s Settings.
