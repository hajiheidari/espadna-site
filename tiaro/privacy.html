Privacy Policy

Tiaro: Daily Logic Puzzle · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Tiaro is a puzzle game made by Espadna ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. The short version: there are no accounts, your game progress stays on your phone, and the app shows ads to stay free.

What stays on your device

Your puzzles, progress, streaks, statistics and settings are saved on your device. The exceptions are the free-hint check, the friends league and the progress backup described below, all under random codes that are not linked to you. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup), if you use it, may include this data. Deleting the app deletes the data on the device; the online backup stays until it expires or you ask us to delete it.

What is collected, and by whom

What we do not collect

  • No sign-in, name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing and challenge links

When you share a result, challenge a friend or invite someone, the app creates a text and link that you send yourself. The link contains the puzzle number, your solving time and a random invite code. It does not contain your name or any contact details.

Free hints for friends who open your links

Free hints are earned when a friend opens one of your links on their own device. To check that, our server (run on Cloudflare) receives two random codes: the code in the link and the random code of the device that opened it. It also keeps a one-way, salted fingerprint of the opener's network address (never the address itself), so that opening your own link, or the same phone or home network counting twice, earns nothing. When your app checks for new hints, it sends your own random code and the same kind of network fingerprint. These codes are not linked to your name, phone number, contacts or any account, and are used only to count free hints. Network fingerprints are deleted after about six months.

Friends league

Opening a friend's link also makes you two friends in the in-app league. To show it, our server keeps, for each random app code: the nickname and animal avatar you choose (shown only to your friends in the league), your brain-score points for the current and the previous week, and which other random codes are your friends. You don't have to choose a real name; nicknames are limited to 16 characters and links are removed. Nothing here is linked to your identity or used for advertising.

Certificates

When you earn a certificate (for example champion of your friends league), our server stores its number, the kind of certificate, the week or day it is for, your league nickname, the app language and your random app code, so the certificate's web page can show it. Anyone with the certificate link can see that page, which is the point of sharing it.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app sends your scores and achievements to Game Center. Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your Game Center identity.

Ads

The only ads are optional videos you choose to watch for coins (in some versions only). The App Store version shows no ads and does not track you.

Backup

So you don't lose your progress when you change phones or reinstall the app, the app keeps a copy of your game progress on our server: puzzles you are playing or solved, streak, statistics, brain-score history, settings, free hints and league nickname. The copy is stored under a random recovery code made by the app (shown in Settings → Backup) and is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any account. Anyone who has the code can restore this progress, so keep it to yourself. On iPhone the code is also kept in the device keychain, so reinstalling the app brings the progress back by itself. A backup that is not updated for 400 days is deleted; to delete it sooner, email us the code.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your device. No notification server or push token is used.

Your choices

  • Ads: change your ad consent choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it), limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings, or buy "Remove Ads".
  • iPhone tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or state laws in the United States), you can ask to access or delete data, or object to processing. Because we do not hold your game data and the analytics and ad data are not linked to your identity, most requests are handled by the services above. Contact us and we will help.

Children

Tiaro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and security

Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months. Ad data is kept as described in Google's policy. Data sent to these services travels over encrypted connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app's "What's new".
